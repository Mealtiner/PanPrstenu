Here we go. From **12:00 today, 18 May 2026**, the [registration form](/en/registrace/) is open to everyone.

Before you dive into filling it out, take this short guide — so you know what to expect.

## Three price tiers

| Tier | Window | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **1st tier** | until 30 June 2026 | lowest — early bird |
| **2nd tier** | 1 July – 31 July 2026 | medium |
| **3rd tier** | from 1 August 2026 until closing | highest |

The exact amounts are in the [registration form](/en/registrace/) and on the [Terms of participation and registration](/en/podminky-ucasti-a-registrace/) page. Children and supported roles (Helpers, Hobbits) have their own discounted rates.

## What's included

- **4 days of participation** (Thursday 20 August – Sunday 23 August 2026), main battle on Saturday
- **Campsite** with fire pits and drinking water
- **Camp game** — shared coins, market, micro-quests
- **Safety services** — medical, security, fire watch
- **Participation in the Saturday main battle**

What's **not** included: food and drink (the [U Zeleného draka pub](/en/prakticky/) is arranged on site), costume, weapon, transport.

## Nine armies, two sides

Before you choose, look at the [Factions page](/en/frakce/), where all armies are listed side by side — colours, heraldry, recommended style, difficulty for newcomers.

- **Free Peoples:** Gondor, Rohan, Elves, Dwarves
- **Forces of Darkness:** Orcs, Uruk-hai, Harad, Umbar
- **Mercenaries:** [the Highlanders](/en/frakce/vrchovina/) — enter on whichever side they please, whenever they please

If you're hesitating, check the **Inspiromat** for that army — that's the [costume photo gallery from past editions](/en/navody-a-inspirace/) per army.

## Roles outside the main game

Not everyone comes with a sword. In the form you can choose from these roles too:

- [Helpers](/en/role/pomocnici/) — event backstage, significantly discounted rate
- [Vendors](/en/role/stankari/) — medieval market
- [Photographers and cameramen](/en/role/fotografove-a-kameramani/) — official documentation
- [Hobbits](/en/role/hobiti/) — non-combat escort, micro-quests
- [Non-combat escort](/en/role/nebojovy-doprovod/) — for parents, partners, people without contact combat
- [Organisers](/en/role/organizatori/) — group leadership, logistics, story

We wrote about each in the [blog post from 8 May](/en/novinky/2026-05-08-pomoc-ktera-prekvapi/).

## For younger participants

- **The main game is from age 12.** Participants aged 12–18 need a legal guardian's consent; 12–15 additionally need a 18+ escort.
- **For younger** we have the [children's game](/en/detska-hra/) — its own rules, its own programme, its own safety framework.
- **Parents:** a short guide is on [For parents](/en/pro-rodice/).

## Before you send the form

Three things you need to know or have ready:

1. **[Rules](/en/pravidla/)** — at minimum the 60-second summary at the top. Submitting the form means you agree to them.
2. **[Terms of participation and registration](/en/podminky-ucasti-a-registrace/)** — the legal part, GDPR, cancellation terms. A bit dry, but fast.
3. **Choosing an army** — browse the [Factions](/en/frakce/) and pick. If you change your mind later, you can — write to us at [info@panprstenu.cz](mailto:info@panprstenu.cz).

## What happens after you submit

1. You get an email with a recap and the payment details.
2. The payment is processed by [Fio API](https://www.fio.cz/) automatically — usually within minutes of arrival.
3. After payment you get a confirmation email.
4. Your name appears in [Who's coming](/en/kdo-jede/), unless you've opted out in your profile.

## Something stuck?

If the form won't fit your screen, doesn't submit, or you get a suspicious email, **let us know** at [info@panprstenu.cz](mailto:info@panprstenu.cz). You're writing to a human, not a robot.

And if you have a general question, try [FAQ](/en/faq/) first — chances are someone has already asked it.

## And now

[Fill in the form.](/en/registrace/) See you at Pulkovský mlýn.
