When someone starts telling you about a LARP, it sounds like a fairy tale. Five hundred people in costume, wooden swords, a Saturday battle in the middle of a meadow. But the real picture only forms once you **see it in motion**.

That's why we have a gallery of nine videos from actual editions on the home page. Here's a quick guide to what to watch first.

## Start with the 2025 documentary

If you've got five minutes for a video, watch the **2025 documentary cut**. It's the newest, highest-quality piece and shows best what actually happens at the event — from arrival through camp life to the Saturday battle.

You'll find it in the [gallery on the home page](/en/#galerie) as the first card marked 2025.

## When you want a longer look

In the gallery there are two videos from 2023:

- **2023 documentary cut** — the long version, an hour of footage from across the event, plenty of atmosphere, plenty of faces.
- **2023 short summary** — a five-minute highlight, great for sharing with a friend you want to tempt along.

If you're torn between "five minutes" and "an hour", start with the short version.

## A historical pocket: 2018, 2016, 2015

Three older recordings show what the Battle looked like years ago. They're worth watching for two reasons:

1. **The aesthetic keeps improving.** Costumes, camera work, audio. But the spirit is the same.
2. **Some situations repeat themselves.** The Saturday Orc raid, the ceremonial Gondor procession, the evening fire in the Elven camp. Tradition is tradition.

## Three trailers for this year

The gallery also holds **three short trailers** for this year's 2026 edition. No plot spoilers — just tension, costumes and music. If you want to quickly show someone _what's coming_, send them one of these.

## How the gallery works technically

So that nothing loads on you for nothing:

- The thumbnails in the gallery are just images from YouTube — no scripts are loaded until you click on a video.
- Once you click, a lightbox opens and **only then** is the player created. That saves your data plan and your battery.
- The modal window respects the site's light/dark variant and opens without animation for people who prefer reduced motion.

The details of how we approach accessibility in general are on the [accessibility page](/en/pristupnost/).
