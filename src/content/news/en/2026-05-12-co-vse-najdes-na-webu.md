The Battle for Middle-earth 2026 website has dozens of pages, and nobody wants to read a sitemap. So here's a thematic guide in six blocks — read it like a menu and click whatever pulls you.

## 1. Rules and the game

What you can and can't do on the meadow, what combat looks like, how costumes are rated.

- **[Rules](/en/pravidla/)** — the full rules with a 60-second summary at the top
- **[Safety](/en/bezpecnost/)** — what to do if something happens, contacts, first aid
- **[Camp game](/en/hra-v-tabore/)** — what happens when there's no battle: coins, market, micro-quests
- **[This year's story](/en/pribeh/)** — what stirs in Middle-earth this year

## 2. The world of Middle-earth

Tolkien as a reference book, not as a test.

- **[Intro to the world](/en/svet-stredozeme/uvod-do-sveta/)** — when you haven't read the books and need a quick orientation
- **[Timeline](/en/svet-stredozeme/casova-linka/)** — what happened when in Middle-earth
- **[Peoples](/en/svet-stredozeme/narody/)**, **[Kingdoms and realms](/en/svet-stredozeme/kralovstvi-a-rise/)**, **[Specific units](/en/svet-stredozeme/specificke-jednotky/)** — who's who
- **[Places](/en/svet-stredozeme/mistopis/)** — the geography of Middle-earth
- **[Glossary](/en/svet-stredozeme/slovnicek/)** — when you stumble on a term you don't know

## 3. Armies and roles

Nine armies, six non-combat roles.

- **[Factions](/en/frakce/)** — nine armies side by side, pick your side
- **[Roles](/en/role/organizatori/)** — helpers, vendors, photographers, hobbits, escorts, organisers

## 4. Before the event: practical info

Logistics, costumes, transport, money.

- **[Practical info](/en/prakticky/)** — what to bring, what to expect from us
- **[Map](/en/mapa/)** — where we are, how to get there
- **[Organisational info](/en/organizacni-informace/)** — schedule, campsite, children's game
- **[Vendors and market](/en/stanky-a-prodejci/)** — who'll be at the market
- **[Guides and inspiration](/en/navody-a-inspirace/)** — Inspiromat for 7 armies, the GoblinTV series, the B5A archive
- **[Registration](/en/registrace/)** — opens 18 May 2026

## 5. Community

People who come, people who organise, people who ask.

- **[Community](/en/komunita/)** — Discord, Facebook, other channels
- **[FAQ](/en/faq/)** — most common questions and answers
- **[Who's coming](/en/kdo-jede/)** — public list of registered participants (after registration opens)
- **[Reactions](/en/ohlasy/)** — what participants say about the event
- **[For newcomers](/en/pro-novacky/)** and **[For parents](/en/pro-rodice/)** — entry points by role
- **[Support participants](/en/podpor-ucastniky/)** — financial support for those who can't afford the event

## 6. After the event and around it

- **[Past editions](/en/minule-rocniky/)** — galleries and short reports
- **[Statistics](/en/minule-rocniky/statistiky/)** — how many of us came and when
- **[Gallery](/en/galerie/)** and **[Photos and video](/en/fotky-a-video/)** — the visual archive
- **[Organiser](/en/poradatel/)** — Moravian LARP, z. s.
- **[For media](/en/pro-media/)** — if you're writing or filming about the event

## And when this isn't enough

- **[Sitemap](/en/mapa-webu/)** — the full page tree
- **[Accessibility](/en/pristupnost/)** — a11y panel, contrast, fonts, reduced motion
- **[Contact](/en/kontakt/)** — when nothing above helped
- **[Five languages](/en/)** — the language switcher is in the header; the site runs in Czech, English, German, Slovak and Ukrainian

## One final tip

If the site feels overwhelming, anchor yourself with three points: **[Rules](/en/pravidla/)**, **[Factions](/en/frakce/)** and **[Registration](/en/registrace/)**. Three clicks, three answers. Add the rest as you need.

And if you stumble on something unreadable, untranslated or just plain weird, **[let us know](mailto:info@panprstenu.cz)**. Every reported bug improves the site for the people who come after you.
