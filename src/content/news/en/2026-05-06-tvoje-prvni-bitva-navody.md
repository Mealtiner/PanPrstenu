You're coming to the Battle for the first time. Maybe someone talked you into it, maybe the theme draws you in, maybe you watched some of the [videos on the home page](/en/#galerie) and are now wrestling with one of three classic questions:

1. **What on earth do I wear?**
2. **What weapon am I allowed to carry?**
3. **What do people actually do on the meadow — apart from the battle?**

None of them is a silly question. Most veterans asked them too. And the site has a concrete answer for each.

## Three guides to start with

If you don't know where to begin, take these three:

- **[Guides and inspiration](/en/navody-a-inspirace/)** — the main starting point. You'll find our own materials and an archive of community guides from [Battle of the Five Armies (B5A)](https://www.b5a.cz/), where costumes are sewn by hand and wooden weapons crafted from scratch.
- **[For newcomers](/en/pro-novacky/)** — a starting point shaped for you specifically. What to expect, how to sign up, what to prepare for.
- **[Rules](/en/pravidla/)** — the full rules for combat, weapons and costumes. They look long, but the 60-second summary at the top will orient you in half a minute.

## The GoblinTV series "How to LARP"

If reading isn't your speed, we have something better. On the guides page we've embedded the **GoblinTV series** — a video show called "How to LARP" that handles the boring part for you.

Nine episodes, each around fifteen minutes, walks you from _"what even is a LARP?"_ through _"how to build your first sword"_ to _"what to do in camp when there's no battle"_. The hosts have been larping for longer than they care to remember and speak like humans — no marketing language.

## Inspiromat: 213 photos for 7 armies

The second weapon for newcomers is **Inspiromat**. It's a photo gallery of costumes from past years, organised by army. Open the page of your chosen army and you see dozens of real costumes that were actually there.

It's not a catalogue of perfect cosplay — it's a sample of what _works_ on the meadow, in the rain, on a Saturday afternoon during the third hour of battle.

Find it by army:

- [Inspiromat — Gondor](/en/navody-a-inspirace/inspiromat/gondor/)
- [Inspiromat — Rohan](/en/navody-a-inspirace/inspiromat/rohan/)

- [Inspiromat — Dwarves](/en/navody-a-inspirace/inspiromat/trpaslici/)
- [Inspiromat — Orcs](/en/navody-a-inspirace/inspiromat/skreti/)

Inspiromat for the Harad and Umbar armies will be added gradually — if you have older photos, [drop us a line](mailto:info@panprstenu.cz).

## If you haven't picked an army yet

Relax. Most people only know after they've read the descriptions. Start with the **[Factions](/en/frakce/)** overview, where you see all nine armies side by side — colours, heraldry, recommended style, difficulty for newcomers.

If you feel combat isn't quite your thing, take a look at [non-combat help](/en/role/) — photographer, cameraman, camp helper, vendor, non-combat escort. We'll write a dedicated blog post about that soon.

## Community: where to plug in

Preparing for your first Battle is faster, and a lot more fun, when you do it with someone. Three places to find both veterans and other newcomers:

- [Community](/en/komunita/) — links to Discord, the FB group and other channels
- [FAQ](/en/faq/) — if your question is one other people have asked, the answer is here too
- [Contact](/en/kontakt/) — when nothing else fits, write to us

## Summary: a 30-minute plan for a complete beginner

If you've got half an hour right now, do this:

1. Watch the **2025 documentary cut** from the [gallery](/en/#galerie) (5 min)
2. Browse the **[Factions](/en/frakce/)** and pick one that pulls you (10 min)
3. Open that army's **Inspiromat** and look at 20 costumes (5 min)
4. Read the **60-second rule summary** on the [rules page](/en/pravidla/) (2 min)
5. Glance at **[Practical info](/en/prakticky/)** — what to bring, how to get there (5 min)

Then decide whether to give it a shot.
