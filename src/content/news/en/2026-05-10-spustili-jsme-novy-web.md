After several months of work, we've finally finished and launched the **new website** for the LARP event **The Lord of the Rings — Battle for Middle-earth 2026**.

If you know us from previous years, it probably won't surprise you that we wanted to push the site forward — from a simple landing page to a living wiki that helps you prepare for the event from the very first thought to the Saturday battle.

## What you'll find on the site

- **[Rules](/en/pravidla/)** — the full rules for combat, weapons, costumes and safety, organised into chapters with search and the 2–4 lives costume rating.
- **[Factions](/en/frakce/)** — nine armies: Gondor, Rohan, Elves, Dwarves, Orcs, Uruk-hai, Harad, Umbar and the Hill-men of the Highlands. Each has its own page with heraldry, recommended costume style and tips for newcomers.
- **[Guides and inspiration](/en/navody-a-inspirace/)** — our own guides plus an archive of community materials from the **Battle of the Five Armies (B5A)** event, an overview of links from [LARP.cz](https://www.larp.cz) and [hurka.uhobitu.cz](https://hurka.uhobitu.cz). Plus the **Inspiromat** — a photo gallery of costumes from past years for each army.
- **[For newcomers](/en/pro-novacky/)** — a complete starting point for first-time participants.
- **[Practical info](/en/prakticky/)**, **[Map](/en/mapa/)** and **[Organisational info](/en/organizacni-informace/)** — where the event takes place, how to get there, what to bring, the schedule, the children's game and the campsite.
- **[Registration](/en/registrace/)** — opens 18 May 2026 at 12:00. The first price tier this year runs until 30 June 2026.
- **[Vendors and market](/en/stanky-a-prodejci/)** — who'll be on site and what you can pick up from them.
- **[FAQ](/en/faq/)** — answers to the most common questions.
- **[This year's story](/en/pribeh/)** — what's stirring in Middle-earth this year.

## What's new on the site

- **5 languages** — the full site in Czech, English, German, Slovak and Ukrainian (translations for the non-Czech languages are being added gradually).
- **Participant journal** — the shared camp game, coins, market and micro-quests.
- **Inspiromat** — a photo gallery from past years for 7 armies, around 213 photos, opened in a lightbox.
- **Army heraldry** — proper heraldic emblems sourced from [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heraldry_of_Middle-earth) and [Tolkien Gateway](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Heraldry).
- **Dark and light theme** — the default is dark, with a light mode you can switch on from the header. Your choice carries over between pages.
- **Accessibility** — adjustable text size, high contrast, a more readable font and reduced motion. All in a floating accessibility panel.
- **GoblinTV series** — the "How to LARP" video series by GoblinTV for complete beginners, embedded directly on the guides page.

## What's next

The site is alive and will keep growing:

- **The 2026 story** — this year's plotline. More soon.
- **Who's coming** — a public list of registered participants (after registration opens on 18 May 2026).
- **Galleries and reactions** from previous years.
- **More languages** — translations rounded out gradually.
- **The children's game in detail** and the programme for our youngest participants.

And of course a **blog**, where we'll keep you up to date on everything happening around the event. This article is the first — but definitely not the last.

## Something not working?

If you spot a bug, a typo in the text or just anything that feels off, please let us know at **[info@panprstenu.cz](mailto:info@panprstenu.cz)**. The site is fresh and every piece of feedback helps.
