On Monday 18 May at 12:00, public registration for the Battle for Middle-earth 2026 officially opens. Veterans and regulars, however, had access to a **pre-registration** a few weeks earlier — and used it more eagerly than we expected.

This evening, on Saturday 16 May, we have **150 participants** on the list. Here's a short look at how they break down.

| Side | Participants | Share |
|---|---:|---|
| Free Peoples | 86 | ████████████░░░░ 57% |
| Forces of Darkness | 58 | ███████░░░░░░░░░ 39% |

Traditionally more people sign up for the Free Peoples — that's a long-term trend. The Forces of Darkness usually catch up in the final weeks of registration, but the Uruk-hai look like they want to be the ones pushing the defenders back this year.

## By age and experience

- **15–25:** ~55% (84 people)
- **26+:** ~45% (66 people)
- **First-timers:** ~22% (33 people)

Almost one in four signed up is here for the first time. A small reminder for veterans: **be kind to them** — even the most annoying first question, who exactly Sauron is, has its value.

## What this means for you if you're waiting until Monday

Three practical points:

1. **There's plenty of space.** 150 of a planned ~500 participants is not capacity. You don't need to sit hammering F5 at 12:00.
2. **Some armies fill faster, though.** If your heart is in Gondor or Rohan, don't wait until June.
3. **The Highlands, Harad and Umbar have room for a whole group.** If you're coming with a crew, take a look at them — the camp atmosphere is a bit different, but no worse.

## When the name list goes public

The [public list of registered participants](/en/kdo-jede/) goes up automatically after Monday's full registration starts. Participants who want to stay hidden have a checkbox in their profile — that's how we respect privacy.

## On Monday

On Monday 18 May at 12:00, [registration](/en/registrace/) opens for everyone. The first price tier runs until 30 June.

If you want to do something useful in the meantime, read through the [Rules](/en/pravidla/) (the 60-second summary at the top is enough), click through the [Factions](/en/frakce/), and look at the [Practical info](/en/prakticky/), so you know what to bring.

See you on the meadow by Pulkovský mlýn, near Rozkoš.
